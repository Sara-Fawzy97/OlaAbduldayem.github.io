Arabic Localization & Campaign Adaptation

From global message to natural Arabic.

Localization Arabic Copywriting Campaign Adaptation

The Brief

Adapt a global home-appliance campaign into Arabic without carrying the structure of the English message word for word.

The Thinking

Keep the original promise of space and flexibility, but move the language closer to everyday life and the way the benefit would naturally be expressed in Arabic.

Why It Works

The adaptation preserves the idea rather than the sentence, turning product features into a more human and relatable outcome.

E-commerce Product Content

Turning product details into copy built for shoppers and search.

E-commerce Product Descriptions Search Content
Anonymized Marketplace Content Structure

Product Title

Creamy Chili Cheese Sauce, 425 ml — Rich, Smooth & Versatile

Key Benefits

Rich, creamy texture with a mild spicy kick. Designed for dipping, spreading, drizzling, and adding flavour to everyday meals.

Long Description

A fuller product story connects texture, flavour, practical uses, and convenience, helping shoppers imagine how the product fits naturally into their everyday routine.

Search Terms

cheese sauce · creamy dip · chili cheese sauce · sandwich sauce · burger sauce · dipping sauce · everyday meals

The Brief

Why It Works

Clear hierarchy makes the listing easier to scan, while benefits-led language turns product details into information that feels useful to the shopper.

05 / 06
Saudi Social Video Script

Turning a product moment into a story.

Scriptwriting Concept Development Saudi Dialect
Scene Excerpt

المشهد: لحظة رومانسية، وهي تنتظر منه الكلام الواثق اللي وعدها به.

هي: يلا حبي، ورّني... إنت الرومانسي الرايق اللي ما يهتز.

هو: نور... قلبي، لبّى عيونك...

هي: هل؟

هو يحاول يكمل... ويتلخبط أكثر.

Product beat:
صوت من خلف الكاميرا: اشرب وكمل... لا تخرب اللحظة.

The Brief

Create a light short-form video concept around emotionally charged moments that begin confidently and unravel under pressure.

The Thinking

Build humour through contrast: high expectations, emotional pressure, and a sudden loss of control. The product becomes the moment that resets the scene.

Why It Works

Instead of interrupting the story with a product message, the product is given a role inside the narrative itself.

Saudi Ramadan F&B Campaign Concept

Turning a seasonal moment into a full brand experience.

Campaign Strategy F&B Saudi Audience Content Concepts
Big Idea & Selected Copy

غبقة على جوك

بعد صلاة التراويح وقبل السحور تحلى الغبقة.

جمع ربعك والغبقة علينا.

ما تروح العزومة ويدك تطوطح.

دش عليهم ببوكس غبقة ترضي جوهم.

The Brief

A self-initiated Ramadan concept proposal developed independently for a Saudi quick-service restaurant brand.

The Thinking

Build the campaign around the Ramadan ghabga: not simply as a meal occasion, but as a familiar ritual of gathering, conversation, and connection.

Why It Works

One cultural insight becomes a flexible campaign system across shared food boxes, social content, competitions, influencer storytelling, packaging, and in-branch experiences.

Product Experience

Shared boxes in different sizes were designed around small and large gatherings, supported by Ramadan-inspired packaging and gifting ideas.

Content Ecosystem

The campaign expands into heritage stories, social challenges, Ramadan traditions, user participation, and content built around gathering moments.

Activation

The proposal also explored influencer storytelling, selected in-branch experiences, competitions, and interactive ideas designed for different audience segments.
